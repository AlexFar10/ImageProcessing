Histograms

  • A histogram counts how many pixels have each gray level 0…255. Positions do not matter.
  • Pixels on the left: dark image. On the right: bright (brightness).
  • Narrow hill: low contrast. Wide: high contrast.
  • Two peaks: two groups of pixels (e.g. sky and grass).

import cv2
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

gray = cv2.cvtColor(cv2.imread("landscape.png"), cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)

hist = np.bincount(gray.ravel(), minlength=256)
print(len(hist), hist.sum(), gray.size)
print(hist.argmax(), hist.max())
print(np.count_nonzero(hist))

plt.bar(range(256), hist, width=1)
plt.show()
